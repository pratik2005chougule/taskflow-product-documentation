# Access and Sign-In

## Prerequisites

Before accessing TaskFlow, ensure that:

- You have a valid user account.
- You have received an invitation to the workspace.
- You have a supported web browser.
- You have an active internet connection.

## Sign In

To sign in to TaskFlow:

1. Open your web browser.
2. Navigate to the TaskFlow sign-in page.
3. Enter your email address.
4. Enter your password.
5. Select **Sign In**.

After successful authentication, the TaskFlow dashboard is displayed.

## First-Time Access

If this is your first login:

1. Open the invitation email.
2. Select the activation link.
3. Create your password.
4. Accept the workspace invitation.
5. Sign in using your new credentials.

## Reset Your Password

To reset your password:

1. Navigate to the sign-in page.
2. Select **Forgot Password**.
3. Enter your registered email address.
4. Follow the instructions sent by email.
5. Create a new password.

## Sign Out

To sign out:

1. Select your profile icon.
2. Choose **Sign Out** from the menu.

## Troubleshooting Sign-In Issues

Common sign-in issues include:

- Invalid username or password
- Expired invitation links
- Locked user accounts
- Network connectivity problems

If the issue persists, contact your administrator or support team.
